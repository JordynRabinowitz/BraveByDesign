ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

Brave by Design

How do educators intentionally build brave, inclusive classrooms—and respond when that safety is threatened?

Three chapters explore proactive design, response to hate, and responsible repair. The finished project should connect each choice to actual EDUC 347 readings and guest lectures.

Important: All scenarios are fictional examples. A later version can add content warnings, accessibility options, audio, and an APA references page. Do not use this prototype as a substitute for school incident-response procedures.
